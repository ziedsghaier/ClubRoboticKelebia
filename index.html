Welcome to Our Community

Welcome to the ISET Kelibia Robotics Club—the ultimate hub for tech enthusiasts, makers, and future innovators! We are a dynamic student-led community dedicated to exploring the exciting worlds of robotics, electronics, and automation. Whether you are a first-year IT student looking to bring your code to life on physical hardware, or a curious beginner wanting to build your very first circuit, our club provides the perfect collaborative environment.

What We Do

  • Practical Workshops: Learn Arduino, Raspberry Pi, circuit design, and 3D printing.
  • Robotics Competitions: Represent ISET Kelibia in national hackathons and contests.
  • Team Projects: Build real, working hardware prototypes alongside your peers.
